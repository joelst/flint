Unstamped replies stay as written when the selected model changes, so switching to Qwen3 does not hide an older answer. A finished reply that starts with ordinary wording such as "Reading" is not treated as a web-status line. Search queries that name a denied IPv6 address are rejected on the device, and a query is listed under Searched for only after it is approved and about to be sent.
